- Natural-image H1 signoff, complete evaluation readiness, real model loss/decoder binding, recovery tests, canaries, training and quality evaluation were not claimed complete.

The next scientific question is: under this measured ambiguity and the existing inputs, how well can the two fixed initializers learn task-conditioned trajectories, retain semantics and satisfy the original offline quality checks? It is NOT whether training can recover a hidden clock exactly from identical observations.

A conditional generator may represent multiple actions for one observation. That possibility does not guarantee that this particular flow-plus-physical-loss objective learns them, that every output is safe, or that a one-frame policy will progress in a closed loop. The full-rollout squared losses can favor a compromise under label ambiguity. This pilot is intentionally allowed to reveal that limitation instead of treating it as already empirically settled.

A mathematical scale check: for two equally weighted position trajectories separated by15.77 mm in H40 XYZ RMSE, their arithmetic midpoint is7.885 mm from each in that norm. This is ONLY an illustrative identity, not a new training label, policy, collision certificate or prediction of attained accuracy. It shows why nonidentity at1e-5 m does not prove failure of a20 mm median quality target. Rotation, tails, all other windows and model optimization must still be measured.

Primary-source context, not project evidence: Chi et al., Diffusion Policy, arXiv:2303.04137, https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.04137, formulates conditional generative action policies with multimodal outputs. That paper does not establish this project's data validity or its model performance.

### 3.2 Quantify an empirical squared-error floor; do not invent a new gate

For an exact-input group C, express all H40 physical position targets P_i in the same frame. Let mu_C be their weighted mean and weights sum to1. Calculate

V_C = sum_i w_i * (1/40) * sum_k ||P_i[k]-mu_C[k]||^2.

This is the minimum empirical mean squared position error for one common deterministic position prediction on that group under the specified weights. An independent random output cannot beat it in expected squared error unless extra identifying information is provided. It is NOT a lower bound on every sample, the median or p95 metric, SO3 error, policy task success, or the entire population distribution. Finite sample/singleton limitations must be stated. Use natural uniform window weights and, separately, the frozen6000-row TRAIN exposure weights when available. Do not infer group statistics from the24,543 pair count alone.

Report this diagnostic and the label spread as numbers; no newly chosen ambiguity tolerance determines admission. A larger-than-expected measured floor remains a scientific finding, not permission to change the recipe. Quantify it and complete the bounded pilot unless it exposes an actual unapproved semantic/integrity defect. Never write mu_C into the training labels. Do not report a label-aware oracle curve as a model.

## 10. Scientific continuation after this first real pilot

This section is a decision roadmap, not extra training or physics authority.

- Both tasks learn and pass original offline criteria: next decision should concern new-family/appearance robustness and a separate frozen-controller transfer test, not more old64 fitting.
- TRAIN fits but KIN-VAL/semantic conditions fail: investigate state/visual/language coverage and shared-task interference; do not infer a solved general model from low TRAIN loss.
- Alias-heavy groups approach their empirical mean-square floor while semantic outcomes are correct: consider a separately preregistered causal-history or progress-conditioned reference design. Any extra deployment feature must be available online and must not be an oracle trajectory index.
- Singleton and aliased groups both fit poorly: first inspect actual optimization/free-rollout objectives and feature use; temporal alias cannot explain all errors.
- Pointwise auxiliary targets appear to suppress valid modes: a future controlled native-distribution versus physical-auxiliary objective comparison may be informative. It is NOT silently added here.
- Good offline motion and bad physics later: isolate controller tracking/runtime/domain shift using the original EEF interface. Do not conflate the present synthetic data with proven WBC behavior.

The immediate priority is the first completed, interpretable matched two-behavior learning experiment. The sources already justify retaining ambiguity as a measured limitation; another unchanged alias existence report cannot replace that experiment.
