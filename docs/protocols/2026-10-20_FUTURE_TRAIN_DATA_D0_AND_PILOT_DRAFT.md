## D1: Bounded Pilot, NOT_AUTHORIZED

Proposed independently registered TRAIN-only version: 24 geometry groups,
each paired T1/T2, exactly 48 first attempts. No unbounded retries or hidden
replacement of valid failures. Reuse existing collectors and audits.

The design crosses 3 appearance/layout families x 2 push directions x 2
distance strata x 2 independent groups per cell = 24 groups. Both directions
must occur in every family; do not reproduce the current F1/-Y confound.
Appearance, direction and geometry fields are explicit, rather than relying
on the existing family-dependent direction generator.

Draft design envelope, not qualified coordinates:

- Same red cube, original 4mm Teacher reference and unchanged task semantics.
- Two distance strata: [0.12,0.15] m and [0.19,0.22] m, separately reported.
- Source X [0.425,0.475] m, lateral center Y [-0.025,0.025] m; goal-X offset
  [-0.012,0.012] m and cube yaw [-0.16,0.16] rad.
- F0/F1/F2 appearance factors crossed with both directions and distances.
- Camera calibration fixed; camera perturbation, new objects, 12mm reference,
  new statistics, event-balanced sampling and recovery are separate variables.

Before any physical GO, freeze exact coordinates/seeds and independent group
IDs in a new manifest. Validate reachability, original safety and exclusion
against existing populations without exposing LOCKBOX observations/results.
If a bound is infeasible, report it before running; do not tune WBC or loosen
S1. A design envelope alone is not a geometric/contact qualification.

Proposed resource ceilings for a separate GO, not presently spendable:
16 CoreLab core-hours, at most 4 single-core workers, 12 wall-hours including
the final hour for closeout, 48 total task starts, 1,536,000 total integrations
(768,000 live + 768,000 shadow), no extra physical qualification/replacement
slots, at most 4232 engine constructions under the mature 88-per-attempt plus
two-per-worker envelope, 48 GiB peak output/staging. Existing per-attempt
768 MiB launcher reservations explain the conservative disk ceiling; the
approximately 2.2 GB successful-demo estimate is not a peak-storage promise.
Zero-step qualification and saved-state audits consume CPU but no extra ticks.
No GPU/PBS/neural queries/optimizer updates. No new owner or overlapping
PV1 resource reservation is implied. Final ceilings require explicit approval.

Stage 1 uses four of the 48 predeclared attempts for collector qualification,
not four additional episodes. Continue only if authority, safety, integrity and
codec binding are trustworthy. Report every planned/attempted/excluded cell,
actual success, no-lift, contact, release/separation, target persistence and
final hold, with original 500Hz/20Hz evidence and two-camera video indices.
Task failures are valid outcomes, not engineering retries. A native safety or
unknown-effect stop prevents automatic continuation.

After the finite pilot, publish qualified independent groups, paired support,
per-stratum success and admission loss, clip/decode rejection reasons, event
exposure and CPU/bytes per admitted independent episode. Then seek a decision
on 64-128 further groups; do not auto-expand based on unused resources.

## D2-D4: Future Decisions

Only an independently authorized new bank may combine qualified old TRAIN
with new TRAIN data. Preserve lineage, processor/statistics and split seals;
do not use DEV/CONFIRM/LOCKBOX for fitting, normalizer calibration or synthetic
success labels. The 21 already-qualified alternate 12mm parents add behavior
variants within already-covered groups, not 21 new geometries.

Compare episode-count learning curves with matched update budgets and
samplers. At the current B_COVERAGE mix, 4096 x 32 gives 16,384 new-T1 and
32,768 new-T2 visits: about 218.45/512 visits per current 75/64 parents, versus
40.96/81.92 if each task grows to 400. These are planned mean visits, not
measured unique coverage or a claim that any count is sufficient.

If a new TRAIN-only normalizer is investigated, both matched arms must use
the same version and pass parent compatibility and raw-label roundtrip gates.
Never hot-refit PV1. Event sampling is a separate controlled intervention;
the prior 1.1647% transition-window exposure is not an entire release-phase
fraction or proof of a causal training deficiency.

Teacher-only CPU rollouts are not on-policy DAgger. Recovery supervision
requires actual student-visited states and an independently qualified expert
that safely completes the remaining task. Existing T2 recovery qualification
cannot be assumed from T1 success or from new CPU demonstrations.
