## Claim boundaries

The long-term goal is a visual-language closed-loop EEF task policy whose task
data and weights can be reused with explicit, replaceable embodiment-specific
execution. Single-controller feedback adaptation and cross-executor / cross-robot
transfer are different experiments. A fixed WBC is a controlled variable within
an experiment, not a demonstrated universal tracker.

The recent offline data domain comprises 30 TRAIN and 10 KIN-VAL local synthetic
geometry families with M1 (target/pose approach) and M3 (directed open-contour
following). Local windows, repeated templates, noise packets and checkpoints do
not multiply the independent scene count. KIN-VAL has informed development and
must not be relabeled as a fresh blind test.

Formal `selected_checkpoint=NONE` and `deployment_selected_checkpoint=NONE` are
retained until an explicit applicable scientific decision changes them.
