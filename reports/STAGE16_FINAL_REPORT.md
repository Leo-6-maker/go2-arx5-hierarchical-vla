## C. Reference lifecycle

The explicit codes are `REFERENCE_COMPLETE`, `TIME_LIMIT`, `FALL`, `INVALID_STATE`, `JOINT_LIMIT`, `TRACKING_ABORT`, `NATIVE_TASK_TERMINATION`, and `UNKNOWN`. Reference completion uses the time-law interval; it is not inferred from fewer than 100 steps and does not use forward-projected `traj_s` alone.

`E2_static_0_0` step 25 was an early `TRACKING_ABORT`: pre-reset `traj_timing_err=0.0445596687` exceeded `0.70 * traj_L=0.0352189329` after grace; `traj_d_lat=0.0467929579` was below its threshold; reference completion, timeout, fall, invalid state, and true joint-limit termination were all false. The 25th post-reset row in the old result had erased this evidence, so Stage16 added a pre-reset snapshot.

Normal reference completion and tracking failure are separate codes. In the 28-case seed-13 suite: 27 `REFERENCE_COMPLETE`, 1 `TRACKING_ABORT`, and zero time-limit/fall/invalid/joint-limit terminations.
