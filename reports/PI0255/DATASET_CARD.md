# PI0255 Motion3 fixed pilot audit dataset

This is an audit dataset of36 failed expert-only episodes from6 scenario families and3 registered motion behavior families. It contains0 successful demonstrations,0 TRAIN/MT-VAL episodes and0 eligible imitation windows. The300 planned collection attempts remain unexecuted because no task passed10/12 pilot admission. The40 future MT-TEST families are metadata only, unrendered and unexecuted.

All36 first-valid failures are preserved:18 settling gate rejections and18 settling physical terminations. No episode entered its actual task phase. The data cannot train or validate motion semantics, establish multitask expert feasibility, or support learned generalization claims. Task failure labels mean failure of the registered preparation prerequisite; they are not observed wrong-route/direction/target outcomes.

Public payload: sanctioned synthetic body/wrist96x96 camera arrays; actual reset/root/DOF/RNG identity arrays; actual desired/achieved/world/base/controller-output telemetry; gate/handoff records; termination events; immutable scene/command/split metadata; independent CPU replay, video manifests and numerical receipts. Private WBC source, controller weights, robot/mesh/URDF assets, compiled caches, credential files and unlicensed external content are excluded. The original WBC MIT notice is retained as attribution; foundation model licence is separate and does not become a dataset licence.

Original24 episodes lack serialized full handoff chunk arrays. They are retained with that evidence deviation; no reconstruction/full gate-codec parity is asserted. Recording V2 adds actual complete chunks for the remaining12 without changing experts, controller, gate or seeds. Saved V2 gates are independently recomputed on CPU. Both commands have identical actual reset state/RNG/initial cameras in each of18 task/family pairs; this proves intervention reset identity, not current observation sufficiency during tasks.

Raw cameras use actual simulation timestamps. Videos include fixed examples for both failure classes and recording versions; presentation10fps is distinct from the original sampling timestamps. Physical termination may auto-reset before post-step camera/achieved readback. Termination event/pre-reset data remain authoritative; post-reset rows are explicitly excluded from clean settling tracking summaries. No subtraction of minima or mixed-frame metric is used.

Scenario-family partitions were frozen before physical stepping. Pilot is excluded from TRAIN/MT-VAL. No captured TRAIN/VAL/TEST overlap exists; empty imitation subsets do not constitute successful data QA. State/action input is the unchanged native XYZ + row-packed rot6d + neutral unexecuted gripper ABI. Policy allowlist is body/wrist images, measured StateV1 and language only. Geometry, expert future, task phase, reward, source IDs and success stay in separate audit/label fields.

TRAIN-derived C_T clipping/support statistics and C_MT scales cannot be measured from zero admitted TRAIN data. Numerical normalization remains absent. No VAL/TEST/pilot statistics substitute for TRAIN. Future matched6000 cards and sampler/RNG CPU fixtures are drafts; actual processor augmentation pairing, tokenizer binding and real Trainer resume remain admission dependencies.
